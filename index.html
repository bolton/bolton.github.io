About

Hi, I'm Rob.

I grew up in Vermont, the first ten years on a sheep farm, and studied economics at UVM. In November 2001 I packed up my car and drove to Snoqualmie Pass to be a ski bum for a year. I was the webmaster for the Summit at Snoqualmie back when that was a real job title, then spent twenty years in Seattle working in tech.

Now my wife, son and I live on the shores of Lake Chelan. I ski tour in the winter, hike with the family in summer, and keep trying to relearn mountain biking in the shoulder seasons. I still love tinkering with the web, which is how beExploring and everything on this page happened.
